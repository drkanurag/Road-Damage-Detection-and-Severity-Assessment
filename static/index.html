ASTM D6433 Pavement Distress Standards

Multi-factor geometric, Black-Hat morphological fissure density, and annular rim-to-core contrast criteria used to grade road damage.

🕳️

Potholes (D40)

Bowl-shaped structural cavity

Formed by asphalt surface disintegration and base course erosion under traffic and moisture infiltration.

  • Low: Diameter < 150mm or area < 0.45%, shallow bowl depth (< 25mm).
  • Medium: Diameter 150–300mm or area 0.45%–1.40%, moderate bowl depth.
  • High: Diameter > 300mm or area > 1.40%, high rim-to-core shadow contrast.
🐊

Alligator Cracking (D20)

Fatigue / interconnected polygons

Interconnected fatigue cracking caused by repeated traffic loading over a weakened or saturated base layer.

  • Low: Fine hairline longitudinal fissures with minimal cross-connection.
  • Medium: Defined polygon network (0.75%–2.20% area) with moderate fissure density.
  • High: Spalled loose polygonal blocks (> 2.20% area, Black-Hat fissure density ≥ 0.28).
📏

Longitudinal Cracks (D00)

Parallel to pavement centerline

Paving lane joint separation, wheel-path load fatigue, or differential subgrade settlement.

  • Low: Hairline width < 6mm, normalized span < 14% with intact edges.
  • Medium: Width 6–20mm, span 14%–32% with minor lip chipping.
  • High: Wide open fissure > 20mm, span ≥ 32% with severe aggregate raveling.
🛣️

Transverse Cracks (D10)

Perpendicular to traffic flow

Thermally induced shrinkage fractures or reflection cracking from underlying concrete slabs.

  • Low: Narrow crack < 6mm spanning < 14% of frame width.
  • Medium: Moderate opening (6–20mm), span 14%–32% with slight roughness.
  • High: Full-lane structural fracture (≥ 32% span) causing perceptible vehicle jolt.
