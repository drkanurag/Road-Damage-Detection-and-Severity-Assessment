ASTM D6433 Pavement Distress Standards

How our AI models grade pavement damage severity according to civil engineering guidelines.

🕳️

Potholes (D40)

Bowl-shaped structural cavity

Formed by asphalt layer crumbling under traffic when subgrade moisture softens base support.

  • Low: Diameter < 150mm or area < 0.4%, shallow depth < 25mm.
  • Medium: Diameter 150–300mm or area 0.4%–1.5%, depth 25–50mm.
  • High: Diameter > 300mm or area > 1.5%, depth > 50mm, sharp rims.
🐊

Alligator Cracking (D20)

Fatigue / chicken-wire pattern

Interconnected cracks caused by repetitive wheel loading on pavements with weakened subgrade.

  • Low: Fine parallel hairline cracks with few interconnecting lines.
  • Medium: Interconnected chicken-wire pattern with minor edge spalling.
  • High: Severe polygon spalling, loose blocks that move under traffic.
📏

Longitudinal Cracks (D00)

Parallel to road centerline

Caused by poorly constructed lane joints, asphalt fatigue in wheel paths, or pavement hardening.

  • Low: Width < 6mm, length span < 14%, intact joint lips.
  • Medium: Width 6–20mm, span 14%–32%, minor edge chipping.
  • High: Width > 20mm, continuous wide fracture with aggregate loss.
🛣️

Transverse Cracks (D10)

Perpendicular to roadway

Thermal contraction fractures caused by rapid temperature drops or joint reflection from underlying layers.

  • Low: Hairline width < 6mm, partial lane span, smooth ride.
  • Medium: Width 6–20mm, partial/full lane span, slight edge spall.
  • High: Major fracture > 20mm, full lane span with vertical displacement.
